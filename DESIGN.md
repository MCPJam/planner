# Planner design

Based on the Paper reference supplied September 14, 2026: near-black canvas, warm white text, coral accent, fine warm borders, compact sans-serif labels, and monospaced data. Colors live only in `design-system/src/tokens.css`.

Keep the product quiet: one heading, one primary action, minimal explanations. Consent uses three selectable rows. The planner emphasizes the week and its commitments. Put connection details behind disclosure. No dashboard metrics, marketing copy, decorative illustrations, or talk instructions in the app.
